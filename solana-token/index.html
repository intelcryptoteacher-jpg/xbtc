XBTC on Solana

DEVNET · Test tokens only · No monetary value

Issue and transfer your own token. This is separate from the Bitcoin BRC-20 XBTC listing. Tokens do not automatically represent ownership of artwork.

1. Connect wallet

Not connected

Use the selected wallet’s browser on your phone. Set it to devnet. Never enter a recovery phrase here. Bitcoin addresses cannot receive Solana tokens.

2. Issue your token

Creates 10,000,000,000 test XBTC with nine decimals in your wallet. Further minting is permanently disabled in the same transaction. No freeze authority.

Requires devnet SOL for fees and account creation. XBTC is the app’s label; wallet name/logo metadata is not included yet.
