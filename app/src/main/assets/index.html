Release checklist

1. Establish the legal operator and exact services offered; obtain an EU legal classification of XBTC and the artwork or metal ownership rights.

2. Verify regulatory authorisation or permitted provider arrangements, including appropriate MiCA CASP permissions and any financial-instrument requirements.

3. Implement identity, AML, fraud prevention, transaction monitoring, sanctions controls, client disclosures, complaints and refunds.

4. Verify seller ownership, asset authenticity, custody, auction terms, delivery and title transfer.

5. Use a real mint address, validated wallet transactions, secure EUR payments, reconciled settlements and documented seller payouts.

A phone call or promise does not establish an issued regulatory authorisation.
